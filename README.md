# 🏠 CheckRooms

### Find Rooms, PGs, Hostels & Flats — Zero Brokerage

CheckRooms is a rental discovery platform built to make it easier for
students and working professionals to find rooms, PGs, hostels, flats,
and shared accommodation without traditional brokerage fees.

The platform connects tenants directly with property owners and provides
a simple way to discover accommodation based on location and property type.

---

## 🌐 Live Website

🔗 https://checkrooms.vercel.app/

---

## 💡 About CheckRooms

Finding a suitable room or PG can be difficult, especially for students
and young professionals moving to a new city.

Common problems include:

- High brokerage charges
- Misleading property photos
- Outdated listings
- Difficulty finding accommodation near universities and IT hubs
- Lack of direct communication with property owners

CheckRooms is designed to simplify this process by creating a direct
discovery platform between property owners and people looking for
accommodation.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make rental discovery simpler, more transparent, and accessible by
helping students and working professionals discover accommodation
without unnecessary broker interference.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔎 Property Discovery

Users can explore available accommodation according to their requirements
and selected location.

### 📍 Location-Based Discovery

The platform is designed around location-based rental discovery, making it
easier to find accommodation close to universities, workplaces and local
areas.

### 🏠 Multiple Property Categories

Users can explore different types of accommodation from one platform:

- Rooms
- PGs
- Hostels
- Flats
- Shared accommodation

### 👤 Owner Listing

Property owners can use the platform to list their available rooms,
PGs and other properties.

### 🤝 Direct Owner-Tenant Connection

CheckRooms follows a zero-brokerage discovery model where users can
communicate directly with property owners.

### ❤️ Saved Properties

Users can save properties they are interested in and revisit them later.

### 🔐 User Accounts

The platform provides separate user flows for people looking for
accommodation and property owners.

### 🌐 Bilingual Interface

The platform provides English and Hindi options for the user experience.

---

## 👥 User Types

CheckRooms supports two primary user journeys.

### 🏠 Property Owner

Owners can:

- List rooms and properties
- Provide property information
- Manage their listings
- Connect with potential tenants

### 🎓 Tenant / Student

Tenants can:

- Search for rooms
- Discover PGs and hostels
- Explore flats
- Save interesting properties
- Connect with property owners
